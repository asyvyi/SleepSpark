Simple Ways to Sleep Better 🛌

Did you know your body sleeps in cycles? Each cycle lasts about 90 minutes. Kids usually need 4–6 cycles per night, which is around 8–9 hours of sleep. Sleeping at the same time every day helps your body stay strong and healthy.

Not sleeping enough can make you tired, cranky, forget things, and get sick more easily. Your brain and muscles need rest to grow and stay healthy.
  • Keep your room dark and cool 🌙 — your body sleeps better in a calm environment.
  • Avoid screens 30 minutes before bed 📵 — phones and tablets can trick your brain into staying awake.
  • Sleep and wake at the same time every day ⏰ — your body loves a schedule!
  • Avoid caffeine in the evening ☕ — no soda, tea, or chocolate too close to bedtime.
  • Relax with reading, drawing, or meditation before bed 🧘 — helps your brain slow down.
  • Exercise during the day 🏃‍♂️ — it helps you sleep better at night.
  • Don’t eat heavy meals right before bed 🍎 — it can make it harder to fall asleep.
  • Drink water earlier in the evening 💧 — avoids waking up at night.
  • Make your bed cozy 🛏️ — your brain associates it with rest and calm.
  • Listen to soft music or nature sounds 🎶 — helps you relax faster.
